Story

Baron Heinrich seeks a trusted knight to secure his family crest and recover the ancestral Baron Signet Ring.

Participants

  • Baron Heinrich in Baron's Estate (Minimum Level: 50).

Steps

  1. Speak with Baron Heinrich at his manor.
  2. Track down the thief who robbed the estate.
  3. Recover and return the Baron's Signet Ring.

Rewards

  • 60,000 XP
  • +300 Karma
  • Baron's Signet Ring
