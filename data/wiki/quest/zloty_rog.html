Story

Bartłomiej speaks of the legendary Golden Bugle whose sound can awaken mountain defenders in times of great peril.

Participants

  • Bartłomiej in Zakopane Heights (Minimum Level: 110).

Steps

  1. Speak with Bartłomiej in Zakopane.
  2. Prove your worth by defeating an archangel or dark fiend.
  3. Gather rare alpine metals and ancient polish wood.
  4. Craft the legendary Golden Bugle.

Rewards

  • 200,000 XP
  • +600 Karma
  • The Golden Bugle (Special Horn)
