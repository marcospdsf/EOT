Story

Hayunn Naratha, the veteran adventurer in Semos Guardhouse, offers to craft a reinforced, specialized Weapon Bag for warriors who bring him the necessary materials, allowing separate organized carrying of weaponry.

Participants

  • Hayunn Naratha in Semos Guardhouse (0_semos_city) (Minimum Level: 10).

Steps

  1. Speak with Hayunn Naratha in the Semos Guardhouse about crafting a #weapon #bag.
  2. Gather the requested crafting materials: 1 Dagger, 2 Iron Bars, and 2 Pelts.
  3. Deliver the materials to Hayunn Naratha.
  4. Receive and unlock the dedicated Weapon Bag interface.

Rewards

  • 500 XP
  • +25 Karma
  • Weapon Bag (Dedicated Storage)
