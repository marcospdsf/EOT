Story

A gentle giant living atop the rolling hills of Kościelisko is being harassed by aggressive mountain rock-throwers and rogue penitents.

Participants

  • The Giant in Kościelisko Hill (Minimum Level: 70).

Steps

  1. Climb the northeastern hill in Kościelisko and speak with the Giant.
  2. Hunt down the hostile mountain penitents causing trouble.
  3. Clear rolling boulder hazards along the ridge.
  4. Return to the Giant to receive his hearty thanks.

Rewards

  • 75,000 XP
  • +350 Karma
  • Giant's Heavy Stone Ring
