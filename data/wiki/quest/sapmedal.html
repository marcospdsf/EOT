Story

The Archer Trainer recognizes supreme precision and agility with the prestigious Sapphire Medal.

Participants

  • Trener Łuczników in Ranger Camp (Minimum Level: 90).

Steps

  1. Demonstrate unerring archery accuracy in ranger trials.
  2. Defeat swift air creatures.
  3. Earn the Sapphire Medal of Marksmanship.

Rewards

  • 120,000 XP
  • Sapphire Medal
  • +400 Karma
