Story

Magician Haizen in his house outside Ados can enchant a dedicated Scroll Bag, granting spellcasters and explorers a protected pouch to safely store and organize their magic scrolls.

Participants

  • Haizen in Ados Magician House (Minimum Level: 10).

Steps

  1. Speak with Haizen in the Ados Magician House about an enchanted #scroll #bag.
  2. Acquire 2 Blank Scrolls (from magic shops/scribes), 2 Small Feathers (dropped by fowls/birds), 2 Pelts, and 2 Silk Glands (dropped by Giant Spiders).
  3. Deliver all materials to Haizen in the Ados Magician House.
  4. Receive and unlock the dedicated Scroll Bag interface.

Rewards

  • 500 XP
  • +25 Karma
  • Scroll Bag (Dedicated Storage)
