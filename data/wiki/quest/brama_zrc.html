Story

The secretive Order of Shadow Knights guards their secluded sanctuary. Prove your courage in the dark to enter.

Participants

  • Shadow Sentry in Shadow Sanctuary (Minimum Level: 100).

Steps

  1. Find the hidden trail to the Shadow Order Outpost.
  2. Speak with the Shadow Sentry and accept the trial of shadows.
  3. Survive the trial without light.

Rewards

  • 120,000 XP
  • Shadow Insignia
  • +350 Karma
