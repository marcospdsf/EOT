Story

Janusz mourns the untimely loss of his beloved wife. He desperately seeks a brave adventurer to retrieve her lost heirloom from dangerous crypt fiends.

Participants

  • Janusz in Zakopane Forest (Minimum Level: 80).

Steps

  1. Speak with Janusz in the outskirts of Zakopane.
  2. Venture into the haunted graveyard catacombs.
  3. Recover the silver locket from the crypt horrors.
  4. Return the locket to Janusz to bring peace to his heart.

Rewards

  • 150,000 XP
  • +500 Karma
  • Janusz's Blessed Amulet
