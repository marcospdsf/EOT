Story

Hone your defensive instincts and resilience to withstand blows that would fell lesser warriors.

Participants

  • Master Defender in Garrison Hall (Minimum Level: 100).

Steps

  1. Fortify your armor and reach 500 Defense.
  2. Present defense training slips to the Shieldmaster.

Rewards

  • 50,000 XP
  • +10 Permanent DEF Bonus
