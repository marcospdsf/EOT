Story

Antithei in Kościelisko charges elite warriors with ascending through 16 grueling dragon trials. Each stage challenges you to defeat legendary dragons solo, unlocking immense draconic power.

Participants

  • Antithei in Kościelisko (0_kościelisko_e) (Minimum Level: 150).

Steps

  1. Travel to eastern Kościelisko and speak with Antithei about your #readiness.
  2. Defeat Stage 1: Red Dragon to prove your martial strength.
  3. Progress through increasingly ferocious dragon species up to Stage 16 (Ancient Infernal Dragons).
  4. Return to Antithei after completing all 16 stages to receive the ultimate reward.

Rewards

  • 1,000,000+ XP
  • +5,000 Karma
  • Draconic Mastery Title
  • Mythic Dragon Slayer Gear
