Story

Banker Kazimierz requires security assistance to inspect the underground bank vaults and establish a regional trade line.

Participants

  • Kazimierz in Zakopane Bank (Minimum Level: 50).

Steps

  1. Speak with Kazimierz inside the Zakopane Bank.
  2. Pay the vault inspection fee and clear subterranean disturbances.
  3. Secure the inner safety lockers.

Rewards

  • 50,000 XP
  • +250 Karma
  • Free Bank Transfer Certificate
