Story

The royal guard at Wawel Castle inspects all entrants. Show deference to the King and prove your loyalty to gain entry.

Participants

  • Royal Guard in Wawel Gate (Minimum Level: 60).

Steps

  1. Approach the royal guard at the entrance of Wawel Castle.
  2. Declare your allegiance and answer the guard's questions.
  3. Bow before the King upon entering the courtyard.

Rewards

  • 35,000 XP
  • Wawel Entry Pass
  • +200 Karma
