Story

A corrupted signet ring threatens to curse its wearer. Seek holy spring waters and mystical essences to cleanse its dark aura.

Participants

  • Wojciech in Kościelisko Meadow (Minimum Level: 90).

Steps

  1. Speak with Wojciech in Kościelisko.
  2. Gather fresh spring water from the high alpine peaks.
  3. Perform the cleansing ritual at the highland altar.
  4. Return the purified ring to Wojciech.

Rewards

  • 100,000 XP
  • +400 Karma
  • Purified Highland Signet Ring
