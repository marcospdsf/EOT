Story

Master smith Józek knows ancient techniques to forge the legendary Złota Ciupaga (Golden Axe with Whisk).

Participants

  • Blacksmith Józek in Kościelisko Forge (Minimum Level: 70).

Steps

  1. Bring a standard Golden Axe and silver bars to Józek.
  2. Gather rare timber from the deep forest.
  3. Assist Józek in tempering the magical blade.

Rewards

  • 80,000 XP
  • Złota Ciupaga z Wąsem (Whisked Axe)
  • +300 Karma
