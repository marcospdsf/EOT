Story

The pinnacle of highland smithing: Józek can upgrade your Golden Axe with a double whisk, turning it into a devastating weapon.

Participants

  • Blacksmith Józek in Kościelisko Forge (Minimum Level: 110).

Steps

  1. Present your Single Whisk Golden Axe to Józek.
  2. Gather dragon blood and celestial iron.
  3. Forge the masterwork Złota Ciupaga z Dwoma Wąsami.

Rewards

  • 200,000 XP
  • Złota Ciupaga z 2 Wąsami (Master Axe)
  • +500 Karma
