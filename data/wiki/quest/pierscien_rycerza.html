Story

Sir Roland of the royal guard offers knighthood and the Knight's Ring to champions who prove chivalry and martial honor.

Participants

  • Sir Roland in Knight's Hall (Minimum Level: 80).

Steps

  1. Undertake the Knight's vow with Sir Roland.
  2. Slay the marauding beasts terrorizing local farmsteads.
  3. Present proof of valor to receive knighthood.

Rewards

  • 100,000 XP
  • +400 Karma
  • Knight's Signet Ring
