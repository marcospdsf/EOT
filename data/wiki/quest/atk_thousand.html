Story

Ascend to elite warrior rank by mastering advanced offensive combat forms up to 1,000 Attack.

Participants

  • Master Swordmaster in Training Dojo (Minimum Level: 150).

Steps

  1. Demonstrate mastery of advanced weapon arts.
  2. Present your tier-2 attack certificate to the Swordmaster.

Rewards

  • 150,000 XP
  • +25 Permanent ATK Bonus
