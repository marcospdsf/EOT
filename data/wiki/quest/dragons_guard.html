Story

An ancient dragon guard blocks the path to the inner vault. Only those who prove their mastery over elemental forces may pass.

Participants

  • Dragon Sentry in Dragon Peaks (Minimum Level: 140).

Steps

  1. Approach the Dragon Sentry at the high mountain gate.
  2. Acquire an Elemental Crystal from cave guardians.
  3. Present the crystal to prove your worth.
  4. Gain passage into the inner dragon domains.

Rewards

  • 120,000 XP
  • Dragon Gate Key
  • +400 Karma
