Story

A magical bridge made of thick mist leads into the clouds above the Tatra mountains, where celestial creatures guard ancient secrets.

Participants

  • Cloud Warden in High Tatra Peaks (Minimum Level: 130).

Steps

  1. Ascend to the highest Tatra cliffside and speak with the Cloud Warden.
  2. Navigate the mist portals to reach the cloud sanctuary.
  3. Overcome the sky elementals guarding the celestial altar.

Rewards

  • 180,000 XP
  • +500 Karma
  • Featherlight Cloud Boots
