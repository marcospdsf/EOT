Story

The Warrior Trainer awards the coveted Obsidian Medal to warriors who prove endurance against lethal subterranean fiends.

Participants

  • Trener Wojowników in Warrior Guildhall (Minimum Level: 90).

Steps

  1. Speak with the Warrior Trainer in the Guildhall.
  2. Eliminate 50 obsidian beasts solo.
  3. Claim your Obsidian Medal of Valor.

Rewards

  • 120,000 XP
  • Obsidian Medal
  • +400 Karma
