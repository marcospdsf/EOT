Story

The Mage Trainer bestows the glowing Ruby Medal upon practitioners of the arcane who master fire and mind trials.

Participants

  • Trener Magów in Arcane Tower (Minimum Level: 90).

Steps

  1. Speak with the Mage Trainer at the Arcane Tower.
  2. Gather 10 pure fiery essence orbs.
  3. Channel the energy to craft the Ruby Medal.

Rewards

  • 120,000 XP
  • Ruby Medal
  • +400 Karma
