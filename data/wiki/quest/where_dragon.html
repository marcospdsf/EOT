Story

Villagers report tremors shaking the mountains. Antithei needs someone to scout the high peaks and find where the primordial dragon sleeps.

Participants

  • Antithei in Kościelisko (Minimum Level: 120).

Steps

  1. Speak with Antithei in Kościelisko about rumors of dragon activity.
  2. Scout the northern ridge for scorch marks and giant claw prints.
  3. Locate the entrance to the hidden volcanic crevasse.
  4. Report your findings back to Antithei.

Rewards

  • 80,000 XP
  • +300 Karma
  • Dragon Tracker Charm
