Story

The merchant burghers award their official signet to dependable traders and adventurers who resolve guild disputes.

Participants

  • Guildmaster Jan in Town Square (Minimum Level: 40).

Steps

  1. Speak with Guildmaster Jan in the central square.
  2. Deliver trading permits and resolve supply shortages.
  3. Claim your recognized burgher status.

Rewards

  • 40,000 XP
  • +200 Karma
  • Burgher's Signet Ring
