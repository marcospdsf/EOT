Story

Young Adaś is terrified because vicious cavern rats have overrun the cellar of his family house.

Participants

  • Adaś in Zakopane Cottage (Minimum Level: 20).

Steps

  1. Visit young Adaś in his house in Zakopane.
  2. Agree to help and enter the dark basement.
  3. Defeat all cellar vermin and eliminate the nest.
  4. Speak with Adaś to give him the good news.

Rewards

  • 15,000 XP
  • +150 Karma
  • Adaś's Lucky Coin
  • Fresh Cheese Wheels
