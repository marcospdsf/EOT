Story

An enigmatic hermit sitting by the highland path tests the wits of passing travelers with ancient Slavic folklore riddles.

Participants

  • Brzezdom in Kościelisko Foothills (Minimum Level: 40).

Steps

  1. Speak with Brzezdom and ask for a #riddle.
  2. Answer his questions regarding local legends, peaks, and kings.
  3. Solve all 4 riddles without failing.

Rewards

  • 45,000 XP
  • +300 Karma
  • Tome of Highland Wisdom
