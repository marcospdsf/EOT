Story

Push your physical offensive capabilities to the absolute limit. Reach 500 Attack power and claim permanent combat enhancements.

Participants

  • Master Swordmaster in Training Dojo (Minimum Level: 100).

Steps

  1. Achieve the required strength and combat prowess.
  2. Bring your attack training slips to the Master Swordmaster.
  3. Receive permanent attack enhancement.

Rewards

  • 50,000 XP
  • +10 Permanent ATK Bonus
