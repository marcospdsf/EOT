Story

The town healer, Gaździna Jadźka, has run out of vital medicinal mountain herbs required to treat injured villagers.

Participants

  • Gaździna Jadźka in Zakopane Town (Minimum Level: 30).

Steps

  1. Speak with Gaździna Jadźka east of the Zakopane Town Hall.
  2. Gather mountain arnica and alpine herbs from the high meadows.
  3. Deliver the fresh herbs to Jadźka before they dry out.

Rewards

  • 25,000 XP
  • +200 Karma
  • 5x Potent Restorative Brews
