Story

Dark tremors threaten to collapse the foundations of Kraków. A scholar warns that an ancient beast awakens below.

Participants

  • Kraków Scholar in Kraków City (Minimum Level: 150).

Steps

  1. Meet the scholar in Kraków City.
  2. Investigate the subterranean fissures beneath the main square.
  3. Defeat the primordial earth fiends causing the collapse.
  4. Seal the rift using blessed runestones.

Rewards

  • 300,000 XP
  • +1,000 Karma
  • Kraków Defender's Crest
