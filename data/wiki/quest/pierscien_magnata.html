Story

Grand Duke Stanislaw commands vast wealth and holdings. Earn his imperial favor to wear the Magnate's Signet Ring.

Participants

  • Grand Duke Stanislaw in Royal Palace (Minimum Level: 120).

Steps

  1. Gain audience with Grand Duke Stanislaw.
  2. Acquire rare precious gems from dragon peaks.
  3. Deliver the tribute to receive the grand title.

Rewards

  • 250,000 XP
  • +800 Karma
  • Magnate's Signet Ring
  • 50,000 Money
